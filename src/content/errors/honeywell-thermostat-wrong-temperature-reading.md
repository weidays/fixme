## What this code means

"Wrong temperature reading" is not a numbered error code. It means the room temperature on your Honeywell thermostat doesn't match how the room feels or what a separate thermometer shows.

The thermostat's built-in sensor reads the air right around the unit. Anything that heats or cools that small spot skews the number. That includes sunlight, a lamp, a supply register, or drafts coming through the wall cavity behind it.

Because the system runs based on this reading, a skewed number causes problems. The system may short-cycle, overshoot the setpoint, or never seem to reach the setpoint.

This condition does not lock out the system, and there is nothing to reset. It clears once the cause is fixed.

## Common causes, ranked by probability

1. **Poor location or local heat sources.** Honeywell installation guides say to avoid mounting the thermostat where these affect it:
   - direct sun
   - lamps, TVs or other electronics
   - kitchens
   - supply registers
   - exterior walls
   - doors and windows
2. **Drafts from the wall opening behind the thermostat.** Unsealed wire holes let attic, basement or wall-cavity air reach the sensor. Honeywell instructs installers to plug this hole.
3. **Temperature offset set incorrectly.** Many models have a display offset (often ±3°F) in the installer setup menu. A previous installer may have changed it.
4. **Normal settling after handling or installation.** Touching the thermostat or mounting a new one temporarily skews the reading.
5. **Weak batteries** on battery-powered models. Low power can cause erratic display and behavior.
6. **Remote or wireless sensor issues** on models that use them. If a remote sensor is set to control the system, the screen may show that sensor's location, not the thermostat's. A wireless sensor may also be out of range. Exact behavior varies by model.
7. **Failed internal sensor.** This is less common and is usually confirmed only after everything above has been ruled out.

## Safe checks before you call anyone

- **Compare against a reference.** Tape a reliable thermometer to the wall next to the thermostat. Wait 15–30 minutes and compare readings.
- **Remove heat sources.** Block direct sun with blinds. Move lamps, TVs and chargers away from the thermostat. Make sure no supply register blows toward it.
- **Check for drafts.** Feel around the thermostat base for cold or warm air coming from the wall. If you feel air, a technician or handy homeowner can seal the wire hole. Turn the breaker off before removing the faceplate.
- **Replace batteries** if your model uses them. Use fresh alkaline cells, not rechargeables.
- **Review the offset setting.** Open the installer or advanced menu following your model's user guide. Check the "temperature display offset" or "temperature correction" value. Set it back to 0 or adjust it to match your reference thermometer.
- **Check sensor settings** on smart and VisionPRO models. Confirm which sensor or room is selected for control, and check the app for sensor connection status.
- **Check the air filter and vents.** A clogged filter or closed registers can make the room feel wrong even when the reading is accurate.

## How a technician will diagnose it

- Compare the thermostat's reading with a calibrated digital thermometer at the thermostat location.
- Check the mounting location and look for unsealed wall penetrations.
- Verify installer settings, including offset, sensor selection and system type.
- Test remote or wired indoor sensors on supported models. Sensor resistance tests are technician-only work.
- Recommend relocating the thermostat, adding a remote sensor, or replacing a thermostat with a failed internal sensor.

Be cautious about any quote for furnace or AC repairs when the only symptom is a display that doesn't match the room.

## Symptom, cause and what to do

| Symptom | Likely cause | DIY action | Technician job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reads high in the afternoon | Sun or a nearby heat source | Close blinds, move lamps or electronics | Relocate the thermostat |
| Reads low in winter | Draft from the wall cavity | Feel for airflow at the base | Seal the wire hole, relocate the thermostat |
| Always off by the same amount | Offset setting | Reset the offset per your user guide | Verify installer setup |
| Erratic readings | Weak batteries | Replace batteries | Check power wiring or C-wire |
| Shows a different room's temperature | Remote sensor is in control | Check the sensor selection in the app | Repair or replace the sensor |
| Still off after all checks | Failed internal sensor | Compare against a thermometer | Replace the thermostat |
